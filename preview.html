Market structure — distribution, clients, regions, workforce

How premium is sold and to whom: the channels and client segments that shape conduct risk and the workforce that carries the market.

Distribution channel, % of premium

SAMA/IA reports 2020–2022. Brokers rose from 34% to 43% in three years; online sales 9.9% in 2022.

Client segment, % of premium

Large enterprises write two-thirds of premium; retail is mostly motor (53% of motor premium is individual).

Geographic region, % of premium

Central region 54% in 2022; Western share fell from 31% to 24%.

Insurance-company employees and Saudization, %

Headcount fell 7% over three years while Saudization rose from 74% to 79%.

Density by segment, SAR per capita

The Authority's five 2026 watch items — and the indicators that track them here

Reading: a growth market with thinning margins — motor loss-making, health margin narrowing, capital buffers down 10 points, complaints up 70% within the year. Sector data can only say so much: the same rules applied insurer by insurer are what turn this into supervision.

Actuarial twin — what if, on the Authority's own numbers

A sector-level twin of FY2025: four segments (health, motor, P&C, protection & savings) with premium, net earned premium, claims and expense ratios, investment income, capital and a synthetic distribution of 25 insurers calibrated to the published solvency bands. Move the drivers, or pick a scenario, and the twin projects FY2026: combined ratios, underwriting result, net income, solvency and how many insurers fall below 125% and 100%.
